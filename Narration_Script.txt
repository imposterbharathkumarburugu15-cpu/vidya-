[SCENE 9: APAAR VALIDATION & CROSS-INSTITUTION DUPLICATE RADAR]
Visual: Central Duplicate Enrolment Radar & Anomaly Dispute Resolution
Narration:
Under the hood, VidyaDwaar continuously safeguards the integrity of admissions across state lines. Through blind HMAC pseudonymization, our cross-institution duplicate radar detects dual enrolments and illegal multi-college seat blocking without centralizing raw Aadhaar or APAAR numbers. When an anomaly occurs—such as a student enrolled simultaneously in two full-time degree programs—the system flags an E203 duplicate dispute for regulatory resolution, stopping fraudulent scholarship claims while safeguarding student data privacy.
